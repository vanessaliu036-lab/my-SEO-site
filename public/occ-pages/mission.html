ABOUT OCC / OUR PURPOSE

MISSION

You may know Robusta. But you may not know Cambodian Robusta.

Give Cambodian coffee a place in the global conversation.

OCC is a Cambodia-origin specialty coffee supplier and Fine Robusta specialist. We focus on coffee grown in Cambodia, connecting the people who buy it with clearer origin information, quality evaluation and practical supply options.

OUR APPROACH

One origin. A fuller story.

We make Cambodian coffee easier to discover, evaluate and source.

01 / CAMBODIAN ORIGIN

Start with the place.

Every coffee OCC offers is grown in Cambodia. We share regional, processing and lot information when it is available for the specific coffee.

02 / FINE ROBUSTA

Judge the coffee, not the label.

Four chapters

01Chapter / OCC

Our Vision

Cambodian coffee deserves to be recognised for the coffees it actually produces, not reduced to assumptions about origin or species.

We want buyers to discover Cambodia through identifiable coffees, named regions and quality information they can assess. The aim is recognition built on substance, one coffee at a time.

Explore Cambodia’s coffee origins ↗

02Chapter / OCC

Our Work

OCC works only with coffee grown in Cambodia. Fine Robusta is our specialist starting point; origin research, sensory evaluation and roast development help us understand what each coffee can offer.

As a specialty coffee supplier, we connect that knowledge with practical buying conversations. We distinguish what is documented from what still needs confirmation, including availability and commercial terms.

Understand Cambodian Fine Robusta ↗

03Chapter / OCC

Why OCC Exists

A compelling origin story may prompt interest. It does not tell a buyer whether a sample represents the coffee on offer, how it was processed, or whether the required quantity is available.

OCC connects the questions that are often treated separately: coffee identity, cup quality, application, supply and the conditions needed for repeat orders. Buyers deserve a clear answer, including when information is not yet available.

04Chapter / OCC

From Origin to Buyer

A café selecting an espresso, a hotel planning its breakfast service and a roaster evaluating a new origin need different things from the same coffee. We begin with the intended use, then discuss the actual product or lot, sample options and cup fit.

Where a commercial offer is appropriate, availability, specifications, volume, packaging and delivery terms are confirmed for that enquiry. OCC-developed coffee follows our wholesale route; a made-to-order roast profile belongs to our roasting program.

BUYER QUESTIONS

Questions,
answered.

01What is the mission of Origin Coffee Cambodia?+

OCC aims to build recognition for Cambodia-origin specialty coffee while helping professional buyers evaluate and source specific coffees. Fine Robusta is our specialist focus.

02Is all OCC coffee grown in Cambodia?+

Yes. OCC’s origin commitment is 100% Cambodia-origin coffee. We do not blend in coffee grown in other countries.

03Does OCC work only with Robusta?+

Fine Robusta is our specialist focus, but our long-term scope can include other coffees grown in Cambodia. Each available coffee and its specifications must be confirmed individually.

04How can a café, hotel or distributor work with OCC?+

Share your intended use, market, preferred format and estimated volume. OCC can then discuss the relevant coffee, sample options and availability; specific commercial terms are confirmed per enquiry.
