Choose the partnership
that fits your business.

If you are exploring a brand collaboration rather than a distribution agreement, start here. Each format below shows who it is for, where the coffee appears and what a typical collaboration includes.

01

Hospitality

Bring Cambodian coffee into the guest journey through service, experience and presentation.

BEST FOR

Hotels, resorts, cafés, restaurants and travel businesses that want a distinctive coffee touchpoint.

COMMON FORMATS
  • Breakfast coffee service
  • Welcome drinks or lobby coffee
  • In-room amenities or guest gifting
02

Retail & gifts

Turn Cambodia-origin coffee into a product people can discover, purchase and take away.

BEST FOR

Concept stores, museum shops, travel retail, gift curation and seasonal brand collaborations.

COMMON FORMATS
  • Shelf-ready coffee products
  • Gift boxes and curated sets
  • Travel or event gifting collections
03

Corporate gifting

Offer a more thoughtful Cambodian gift for teams, clients, events and business relationships.

BEST FOR

Companies, conferences, business events, partner appreciation and internal gifting programs.

COMMON FORMATS
  • Client and partner gift sets
  • Event welcome kits
  • Custom quantity requests with presentation guidance

Need something outside these three formats? We also support limited creative collaborations and can adapt the proposal according to market, quantity, packaging direction and timeline.
