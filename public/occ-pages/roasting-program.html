02 · How it works

From brief to approved roast profile.

The process is deliberately simple: define the product, roast against the brief, evaluate it in context, then approve a commercial reference.

01

Brief

Tell us the target customer, brewing method, equipment, service context, and the experience the coffee should create.

02

Sample Roast

We select the coffee and develop roast directions against the commercial brief rather than a generic roast label.

03

Evaluate

Cup and brew the samples in the intended application, focusing on sweetness, body, structure, finish, and performance.

04

Approve

The chosen direction becomes the reference for production and future quality conversations.

Reference

Before you contact us.

Do I need to know the roast profile already?

No. A clear product goal, customer context, and brewing application are enough to begin. The development process translates those requirements into an evaluable roast direction.

Can Fine Robusta be part of the program?

Yes, when it suits the intended product. OCC evaluates the actual coffee, processing, sensory character, application, and roast direction together.

How is this different from OCC Wholesale?

Wholesale is for OCC-developed, ready-to-sell coffee profiles. Custom Roasting is for partners who need a profile or product direction developed around their own market and use case.
