Cambodian coffee.Rooted in place.

From the highlands of Mondulkiri to the cup, discover the places, growing conditions and people behind OCC coffee.

Our coffee is grown in Cambodia. Origin means where the coffee was cultivated—not simply where it was roasted or packed. Mondulkiri is our central regional focus; individual farms and lots tell the more specific story.

Explore deeper / 01—03

Three ways to go deeper.

Start with the region, move closer to the farm, then follow quality into the cup. Each guide owns one part of the story so this page can stay clear and focused.

01 / Where it begins

The map starts in Mondulkiri.

OCC's regional focus is Mondulkiri in northeastern Cambodia. It is a place to begin understanding Cambodian coffee, not a promise that every farm or lot will taste alike. Explore the wider geography, including Ratanakiri, in our regional guide.

Explore Cambodia & Regions ↗
02 / How it grows

Closer to the coffee tree.

Soil, climate, shade, water and farming decisions shape the conditions in which coffee grows. Those factors change from farm to farm. Our Farm & Terroir guide looks beyond the province name and into the growing environment.

Explore Farm & Terroir ↗
03 / What reaches the cup

Discover the other side of Robusta.

OCC specializes in Fine Robusta, but quality is evaluated coffee by coffee—not guaranteed by the word Robusta or a place name. Harvest selection, processing and sensory evaluation help explain what a particular lot can offer.

Discover Fine Robusta Cambodia ↗
Origin / The proof behind the story

A place name starts the story.
Evidence gives it meaning.

We describe what can be supported for each coffee. Country and region create context; producer, harvest, process and lot information add precision when verified. We do not invent missing details.

Three questions behind every origin claim
01

Where did it grow?

What happened after harvest?

03

Keep tasting notes and quality statements tied to the coffee evaluated.

Specific evidence is provided only where it is available and verified.
